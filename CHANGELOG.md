# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- "Arrange a shipment to one of your workshops" in the town menu: load any goods, pick one of your workshop towns and pay a carter.
- A guarded caravan carries the cargo to that town's workshop warehouse. It never trades on the way, but enemies can attack it and take the cargo.
- Travel times in days and hours, learned from how fast your shipments really move.
- Three escorts: Basic guard (12 men), Armed escort (25) and Veteran escort (40).
- Cargo that doesn't fit in the warehouse is sold at that town's market.
- Shipments whose destination becomes unreachable head to your nearest other warehouse, or sell the cargo in the nearest friendly town.
- "Check shipments on the road" panel with a card per shipment: picture of its biggest cargo item, route, cargo, arrival time, escort and status.
- Mod Configuration Menu options for fees, escorts, the shipment limit, showing where shipments are, and debug messages.
